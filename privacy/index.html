This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile and desktop application Jawhar Xoshnaw (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The App is a contracting / project management tool used to record projects, expenses, payments, workers, reports, and optional backups.

1. Who we are

The App is provided for business use related to contracting operations. For privacy questions or account requests, contact us via WhatsApp: +964 750 232 1637 (wa.me/9647502321637) or through girfan.xyz.

2. Information we collect

The App is designed to work primarily offline on your device.

  • Account credentials you enter (username/password) — stored locally on the device to keep you signed in after the first login.
  • Business data you enter, such as project details, expenses, payments, worker information, notes, exchange rates, and related records.
  • Photos / images you choose to attach to general expenses (camera or photo library). These are compressed and stored locally on the device.
  • Backup files you create (database + expense images). Backups remain under your control on the device unless you choose to export or send them.
  • Optional Telegram backup configuration (bot token and chat ID) if you enable Telegram backups. These values are stored locally and used only to send your backup file to the chat/channel you configure.
  • Device identifiers used for licensing/trial on supported platforms (to determine trial/license status on that device). This is not used for advertising.

We do not require you to create an online profile with us to use the core offline features. Account access for distribution builds may be provided after contacting us (for example via WhatsApp).

3. How we use information

  • To provide App features (projects, expenses, payments, reports, backups).
  • To keep you logged in after successful authentication.
  • To attach and display expense images you add.
  • To create and restore backups you request.
  • To send optional backups to Telegram only if you configure and enable that feature.
  • To manage trial/license access on the device.

4. Data storage and sharing

  • Local storage: Business data and images are stored on your device (application storage / local database).
  • No sale of data: We do not sell your personal or business data.
  • No advertising trackers: The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs for personalized ads.
  • Telegram (optional): If you enable Telegram backup, backup files are transmitted to Telegram’s servers using the credentials you provide, under Telegram’s own terms and privacy practices.
  • WhatsApp (optional contact): If you contact us for account setup, the conversation happens in WhatsApp under Meta/WhatsApp policies.
  • Apple / Google / device OS: Standard platform services may process technical information as part of installing and running the App.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — to capture expense images (when you choose).
  • Photo library — to select expense images (when you choose).
  • Network — for optional features such as Telegram backup and opening WhatsApp/website links.
  • File / storage access — as needed to save or restore backups and images on supported platforms.

Permissions are used only for the stated features and are not required beyond that purpose.

6. Account deletion

You can delete your account and local App data from within the App: Menu → Delete Account (سڕینەوەی هەژمار). This signs you out and permanently removes local business data and expense images stored by the App on that device. This action cannot be undone unless you restore from a backup you previously saved.

You may also request help by contacting WhatsApp +964 750 232 1637.

7. Data retention

Data remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the App, wipe the device, or use Delete Account. Backup files you exported or sent to Telegram remain wherever you stored or sent them, under your control.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for business / contracting use and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction).

9. Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate for a primarily offline app, including local storage on the device. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Protect your device, backups, and any Telegram credentials you configure.

10. International users

The App may be used in different regions. Because data is mainly stored on your device, processing generally occurs on that device unless you use optional online features (such as Telegram backup or contacting support).
